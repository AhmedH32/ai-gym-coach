# ID: PROG_VOLUME_LANDMARKS
**Topic:** Muscle-Specific Volume Landmarks (MV, MEV, MAV, MRV)
**Category:** Volume
**Evidence Base:** Schoenfeld BJ, Ogborn D, Krieger JW. (2017, PMID: 27433960); Israetel M, Hoffmann J. (2020, Scientific Principles of Hypertrophy Training).

## Volume Landmark Definitions
- **Maintenance Volume (MV):** ~4-6 hard sets/week per muscle group required to preserve current muscular adaptations.
- **Minimum Effective Volume (MEV):** ~6-8 hard sets/week to elicit measurable hypertrophy.
- **Maximum Adaptive Volume (MAV):** ~12-18 hard sets/week (the optimal stimulus-to-fatigue zone for maximum progress).
- **Maximum Recoverable Volume (MRV):** ~20-24 hard sets/week (exceeding leads to non-functional overreaching, fatigue accumulation, and strength regression).
