# ID: PROG_SPLIT_DESIGN
**Topic:** Training Frequency, Split Architecture, and Session Distribution
**Category:** Periodization
**Evidence Base:** Schoenfeld BJ, Ogborn D, Krieger JW. (2016, PMID: 27102172); Helms ER et al. (2019, The Muscle and Strength Pyramid).

## Core Split Archetypes
- **Full Body (3x/week):** 1 multi-joint compound per primary movement pattern per workout (e.g., Squat/Hinge/Push/Pull). Ideal for novices (high motor-skill frequency) or lifters with <=3 days/week availability.
- **Upper / Lower (4x/week):** 2 Upper body sessions, 2 Lower body sessions per 7-day microcycle. Delivers 2x/week frequency per muscle group with 48-72h inter-session recovery. Optimal baseline for intermediate hypertrophy.
- **Push / Pull / Legs (PPL - 5-6x/week):** Push (Chest, Anterior/Lateral Delts, Triceps), Pull (Back, Rear Delts, Biceps), Legs (Quads, Hamstrings, Calves). Allows high volume concentration per muscle group. Requires advanced recovery management.

## Frequency and Volume Distribution Rules
- Hypertrophy is significantly greater when muscle groups are trained at least twice per week compared to once per week on a volume-equated basis (Schoenfeld et al., 2016).
- **Per-Session Volume Ceiling:** Cap hard working sets for a single muscle group at 8-10 sets per session. Any sets performed past 10 in a single workout suffer diminished stimulus and produce excess fatigue ("junk volume").
