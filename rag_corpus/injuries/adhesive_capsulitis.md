# ID: COND_ADHESIVE_CAPSULITIS

**Condition Name:** Adhesive Capsulitis (Frozen Shoulder)

**Category:** Shoulder

**Mechanism of Irritation:** Fibrotic thickening and contracture of the glenohumeral joint capsule, restricting active and passive range of motion across all planes, particularly external rotation and abduction.

## Colloquial Symptom Presentation & Provocative Triggers
* **Anatomical Pain Sites:** Deep inside shoulder joint, anterior capsule, side of shoulder, deltoid insertion.
* **Sensation Character:** Deep throbbing ache, sharp catch at end-range, frozen stiffness, night toothache pain.
* **Provocative Gym Lifts & Angles:** Overhead barbell press at lockout, snatch-grip overhead positions, end-range bench press bottom, behind-the-neck pulldowns, dips past 90 degrees shoulder extension.
* **Gym-Floor Slang Cues:** "My shoulder feels like it's locked in concrete", "sharp grabbing pain when I try to reach overhead", "can't unpack my shoulder out of the bottom of a bench".

## Absolute Contraindications (Exercises to Avoid)

* Aggressive end-range ballistic shoulder stretching under heavy load
* Heavy overhead barbell presses requiring full active ROM
* Kipping pull-ups or explosive hanging movements

## Biomechanical Modifications (Rules)

* Perform strength exercises strictly within pain-free available range of motion
* Utilize submaximal isometric contractions near available ROM boundaries
* Maintain neutral shoulder positioning and avoid forced end-range leverage

## Recommended Substitutions

* Low-cable standing rows within comfortable ROM instead of heavy barbell rows
* Dumbbell chest-supported press restricted to painless ROM
* Scapular protraction/retraction drills (push-up plus) in neutral shoulder position
