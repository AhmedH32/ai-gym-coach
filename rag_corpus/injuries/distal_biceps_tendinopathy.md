# ID: COND_DISTAL_BICEPS_TENDINOPATHY

**Condition Name:** Distal Biceps Tendinopathy

**Category:** Elbow

**Mechanism of Irritation:** Degenerative changes and micro-tears at the distal insertion of the biceps brachii tendon onto the radial tuberosity, aggravated by heavy eccentric loading in supination.

## Colloquial Symptom Presentation & Provocative Triggers
* **Anatomical Pain Sites:** Front of the elbow crease, inside elbow bend, distal biceps insertion, top of forearm near elbow
* **Sensation Character:** Deep throbbing ache under load, sharp bite when stretching arm straight, hot burning stiffness post-workout
* **Provocative Gym Lifts & Angles:** Bottom of heavy chin-ups, mixed-grip deadlifts on the underhand side, preacher curls in full elbow extension, heavy supinated dumbbell curls
* **Gym-Floor Slang Cues:** "My elbow crease is screaming at the bottom of curls", "sharp pinch inside my elbow when I unrack deadlifts", "feels like my biceps tendon is going to snap straight"

## Absolute Contraindications (Exercises to Avoid)

* Heavy eccentric biceps curls into full elbow extension lock-out
* Overhand mixed-grip deadlifts on the supinated arm side
* Ballistic heavy chin-ups with rapid bottom drop-out

## Biomechanical Modifications (Rules)

* Avoid full passive elbow extension lock-out under load
* Perform deadlifts with double-overhand grip + straps or hook grip to avoid asymmetrical biceps load
* Use neutral hammer grip to reduce tensile stress on radial tuberosity insertion

## Recommended Substitutions

* Hammer curls stopping 10 degrees short of full elbow lock-out
* Double-overhand strap deadlifts instead of mixed-grip deadlifts
* Cable neutral-grip pulldowns with controlled tempo
