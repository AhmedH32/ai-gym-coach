# ID: COND_LATERAL_ANKLE_SPRAIN

**Condition Name:** Lateral Ankle Sprain

**Category:** Ankle

**Mechanism of Irritation:** Inversion and plantarflexion trauma causing tearing or stretching of the lateral ankle complex ligaments (anterior talofibular ligament ATFL, calcaneofibular ligament CFL).

## Colloquial Symptom Presentation & Provocative Triggers
* **Anatomical Pain Sites:** outside of ankle, lateral malleolus, outer ankle bone, front-outside ankle joint line (ATFL site)
* **Sensation Character:** sharp rolling pain, unstable catching feeling, dull outer ankle throb, weak giving-way sensation
* **Provocative Gym Lifts & Angles:** lateral bounding or box jumps, loaded walking lunges with ankle inversion, deep heavy squats with ankles rolling out, deficit split squats
* **Gym-Floor Slang Cues:** "my ankle keeps rolling outwards on lunges", "feels like my outer ankle is going to give out", "sharp pain on the outside of my ankle when changing directions"

## Absolute Contraindications (Exercises to Avoid)

* Single-leg agility ladder drills on uneven surfaces during acute recovery
* Loaded split squats with foot rolling into inversion
* Plyometric lateral jumping without ankle bracing/taping

## Biomechanical Modifications (Rules)

* Perform standing exercises with foot flat and stable ground contact
* Incorporate ankle semi-rigid bracing or taping during heavy standing lifts
* Focus on proprioceptive balance training on firm flat surfaces before unstable surfaces

## Recommended Substitutions

* Bilateral standing calf raises instead of single-leg unstable balance drills
* Seated leg press or machine squat instead of dynamic walking lunges
* Peroneal eversion isometric strengthening against resistance band
