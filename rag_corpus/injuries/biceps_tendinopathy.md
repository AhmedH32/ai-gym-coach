# ID: COND_BICEPS_TENDINOPATHY

**Condition Name:** Biceps Tendinopathy (Long Head)

**Category:** Shoulder

**Mechanism of Irritation:** Repetitive traction or friction of the long head of the biceps tendon within the bicipital groove of the humerus, often secondary to glenohumeral instability or anterior impingement.

## Colloquial Symptom Presentation & Provocative Triggers
* **Anatomical Pain Sites:** Front of shoulder, bicipital groove, upper arm, tendon in shoulder crease
* **Sensation Character:** Sharp pinpoint pain, deep throbbing ache, burning under tension, catching sensation
* **Provocative Gym Lifts & Angles:** Bottom of incline dumbbell curls with deep extension, loaded supinated chin-ups at full lockout, deep bench press, preacher curls past 90 degrees
* **Gym-Floor Slang Cues:** "My front shoulder is screaming at the bottom of a curl", "feels like a rope popping in the front of my joint", "stabbing pain when I uncork my arms on chin-ups"

## Absolute Contraindications (Exercises to Avoid)

* Preacher curls with hyper-extended elbows
* Incline dumbbell curls with excessive shoulder extension
* Heavy underhand (supinated) chin-ups extending to full arm lock-out

## Biomechanical Modifications (Rules)

* Maintain neutral wrist/forearm position (hammer grip) during elbow flexion movements
* Avoid deep shoulder extension during curls and pulling movements
* Limit shoulder anterior translation during pressing and pulling

## Recommended Substitutions

* Neutral-grip hammer curls instead of supinated preacher curls
* Neutral-grip cable pulldowns instead of underhand supinated chin-ups
* Rope cable hammer curls with controlled range of motion
