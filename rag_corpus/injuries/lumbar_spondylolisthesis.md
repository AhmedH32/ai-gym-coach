# ID: COND_LUMBAR_SPONDYLOLISTHESIS

**Condition Name:** Lumbar Spondylolisthesis

**Category:** Back

**Mechanism of Irritation:** Anterior displacement of one vertebral body relative to the inferior vertebra (often secondary to pars interarticularis defect), aggravated by lumbar hyperextension and high shear forces.

## Colloquial Symptom Presentation & Provocative Triggers
* **Anatomical Pain Sites:** low back, belt line, lumbar spine, top of the buttocks
* **Sensation Character:** deep catching ache, dull spinal ache, instability feeling, sharp catch on arching
* **Provocative Gym Lifts & Angles:** standing overhead press with back arching, heavy back squats with excessive lumbar extension, standing barbell rows, hyper-extended bench press setups
* **Gym-Floor Slang Cues:** "my lower back feels like it's sliding out", "sharp catch when I arch my lower back", "my back pinches when overhead pressing standing up"

## Absolute Contraindications (Exercises to Avoid)

* Lumbar hyperextension exercises (e.g., cobra pose, standing back extensions)
* Heavy axial compressive loading with spinal extension (e.g., heavy overhead press standing)
* High-impact plyometrics with spinal arching

## Biomechanical Modifications (Rules)

* Maintain a strict neutral-to-slightly flexed lumbar alignment
* Prioritize abdominal bracing and anterior core strength to prevent lumbar shear
* Use chest-supported or seated loading positions to minimize axial spinal shear

## Recommended Substitutions

* Dead-bug core bracing drills instead of back extension machines
* Chest-supported dumbbell row instead of standing bent-over barbell row
* Seated cable shoulder press with back supported instead of standing barbell press
