# ID: PROG_REP_RANGES
**Topic:** Repetition Ranges, Proximity to Failure (RIR/RPE), and Rest Intervals
**Category:** Intensity
**Evidence Base:** Schoenfeld BJ et al. (2021, PMID: 33430459); Zourdos MC et al. (2016, PMID: 26439782); de Salles BF et al. (2009, PMID: 19691365).

## Repetition & Stimulus Zones
- **Maximum Strength / Neural Adaptation (3-6 reps @ RPE 8-9 / 1-2 RIR):** Primary driver: mechanical tension, motor unit recruitment, and rate coding. Rest periods: 3.0-5.0 minutes. Strictly reserved for Tier 1 multi-joint compounds.
- **Hypertrophy / Mechanical Tension (6-12 reps @ RPE 7-9 / 1-3 RIR):** Primary driver: high mechanical tension balanced with volume efficiency. Rest periods: 1.5-3.0 minutes. Ideal for Tier 1 and Tier 2 compounds.
- **Metabolic Stress / Localized Hypertrophy (12-20 reps @ RPE 8-10 / 0-2 RIR):** Primary driver: cellular swelling, metabolite accumulation, and sarcoplasmic stimulus. Rest periods: 60-90 seconds. Ideal for Tier 3 isolation and machine exercises.

## Proximity to Failure (RIR / RPE Rules)
- **Multi-Joint Free Weight Compounds:** Terminate sets at 1-3 RIR (RPE 7-9) to maintain technique integrity and limit axial CNS fatigue.
- **Single-Joint Machine & Cable Isolation:** Safe to take to 0 RIR (RPE 10 / momentary technical failure) without elevating injury risk or systemic overtraining.
